Query: Open dataset of hourly validated NO2 and PM2.5 measurements from Italian urban air quality monitoring stations covering March 2004 to April 2005, for example EEA AirBase or a regional ARPA open data portal. Give the exact dataset page URL and the years it covers.

An open dataset providing hourly validated $\text{NO}_2$ and $\text{PM}_{2.5}$ measurements from Italian urban monitoring stations covering March 2004 to April 2005 is **EEA AirBase (Version 8)**:

### **Dataset Details**
* **Dataset:** AirBase – The European air quality database (Version 8)
* **Dataset Page URL:** [https://www.eea.europa.eu/data-and-maps/data/airbase-the-european-air-quality-database-8](https://www.eea.europa.eu/data-and-maps/data/airbase-the-european-air-quality-database-8)
* **Years Covered:** **1969 – 2012** (systematic, dense monitoring across Italian urban and regional networks is available from the late 1990s through December 31, 2012, directly encompassing March 2004 to April 2005).

---

### **How to Access the Data**
1. On the AirBase v8 dataset page under the **Data by country** tab, select **Italy (IT)**.
2. The downloaded archive provides individual station time-series files (containing hourly validated measurements and validation quality flags) classified by site typology, including **Urban Traffic** and **Urban Background** stations, for both nitrogen dioxide ($\text{NO}_2$, pollutant code 8) and fine particulate matter ($\text{PM}_{2.5}$, pollutant code 6001).

*(Note: Starting with reporting year 2013 onwards, AirBase was succeeded by the EEA's [Air Quality e-Reporting (AQER)](https://www.eea.europa.eu/data-and-maps/data/aqereporting-8) system, while the historical pre-2013 data continues to be hosted via the AirBase archives).*
