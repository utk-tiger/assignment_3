### 3. Data Validation Status for 2024 and 2025: E1a vs. E2a

Under EU Directive 2008/50/EC and EEA reporting rules, data streams are split into two flows:
* **E2a:** Up-to-date (UTD) / near-real-time measurements transmitted continuously by national monitoring authorities (e.g., ISPRA/ARPA in Italy) that are **unvalidated**.
* **E1a:** Official **validated** assessment data, rigorously quality-controlled and submitted annually by Member States by **September 30 of year $t+1$ for year $t$**.

#### Breakdown by Year:
* **2024 Data:**  
  * **Validated (E1a):** The deadline for Member States to report validated 2024 data was **September 30, 2025**. Full-year 2024 data for Italy is available as **E1a** (Dataset 2 in the download service).
* **2025 Data:**  
  * **Up-to-date unvalidated (E2a):** The official E1a submission deadline for calendar year 2025 is **September 30, 2026**. Until national validation and EEA ingestion are complete, recent 2025 data is accessed primarily via **E2a** (Dataset 1 in the download service). Once validated submissions are processed, E1a replaces E2a for that calendar year.
