Query: Open data hourly air temperature and relative humidity observations from ARPA weather stations in northern Italy (Veneto or Lombardy) for 2004-2005 and 2024-2025: exact dataset page URL, licence and period covered.

For open data hourly observations of **air temperature** (*temperatura dell'aria*) and **relative humidity** (*umidità relativa*) spanning both historical (2004–2005) and recent (2024–2025) periods, **ARPA Lombardia** provides complete coverage. 

*(Note: While ARPA Veneto provides open data, its online hourly automated download portal only starts from January 1, 2010; ARPA Lombardia covers both periods).*

---

### ARPA Lombardia (Lombardy)

#### 1. Exact Dataset Page URLs
ARPA Lombardia publishes its meteorological data on the official Open Data portal of Regione Lombardia and through its dedicated open extraction service:

* **Regional Open Data Hub (ARPA Lombardia Monitoring):**  
  [https://www.dati.lombardia.it/stories/s/auv9-c2sj](https://www.dati.lombardia.it/stories/s/auv9-c2sj)  
  *(Under the section **Idro-Nivo-Meteo → Dati Storici**, individual datasets are partitioned by variable and time period: `Temperatura fino al 2010`, `Temperatura dal 2021`, `Umidità Relativa fino al 2010`, and `Umidità Relativa dal 2021`).*
* **Live / Rolling Window Sensor Stream:**  
  [https://www.dati.lombardia.it/Ambiente/Dati-sensori-meteo/647i-nhxk](https://www.dati.lombardia.it/Ambiente/Dati-sensori-meteo/647i-nhxk)
* **ARPA Lombardia Official Direct Data Service (*Richiesta Dati Misurati*):**  
  [https://www.arpalombardia.it/temi-ambientali/meteo-e-clima/richiesta-dati-misurati/](https://www.arpalombardia.it/temi-ambientali/meteo-e-clima/richiesta-dati-misurati/)  
  *(Enables automated extraction of full multiannual hourly and sub-hourly series for any sensor directly into CSV format).*

#### 2. Licence
* **Creative Commons 1.0 Universal (CC0 1.0 - Public Domain Dedication)** on `dati.lombardia.it`.

#### 3. Period Covered
* **From station installation (late 1980s / 1990s / early 2000s depending on the station) to present (updated continuously)**:
  * **2004–2005**: Covered under the *fino al 2010* historical archive.
  * **2024–2025**: Covered under the *dal 2021* archive and the rolling/near-real-time sensor dataset.

### Alternative: ARPA Veneto (ARPAV)
If working with Veneto data:
* **Hourly Data Tool:** [ARPA Veneto Dati meteorologici orari](https://www.arpa.veneto.it/temi-ambientali/meteo-idro-nivo/dati-meteorologici-orari)
* **Licence:** Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (**CC BY 4.0**).
* **Period Covered:** **January 1, 2010 to present**. *(Veneto covers 2024–2025 hourly, but 2004–2005 is only available online as monthly/annual aggregates, requiring a direct formal archive request to ARPAV for sub-2010 hourly series).*
