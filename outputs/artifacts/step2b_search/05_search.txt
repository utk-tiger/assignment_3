Query: Open data hourly road traffic vehicle counts from induction loop counters in an Italian city (for example Milan, Bologna or Verona) covering 2024 and 2025: exact dataset page URL, licence and period.

The open data portal for **Bologna, Italy** (*Comune di Bologna*) publishes road traffic counts from induction loop counters (*spire a induzione*) broken down hourly into annual datasets:

---

### **1. 2024 Dataset**
* **Dataset Name:** *Rilevazione flusso veicoli tramite spire - anno 2024*
* **Exact Dataset Page URL:** [https://opendata.comune.bologna.it/explore/dataset/rilevazione-flusso-veicoli-tramite-spire-anno-2024/information/](https://opendata.comune.bologna.it/explore/dataset/rilevazione-flusso-veicoli-tramite-spire-anno-2024/information/)
* **Licence:** Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (**CC BY 4.0**)
* **Period Covered:** January 1, 2024 – December 31, 2024 (hourly vehicle count per sensor)

---

### **2. 2025 Dataset**
* **Dataset Name:** *Rilevazione flusso veicoli tramite spire - anno 2025*
* **Exact Dataset Page URL:** [https://opendata.comune.bologna.it/explore/dataset/rilevazione-flusso-veicoli-tramite-spire-anno-2025/information/](https://opendata.comune.bologna.it/explore/dataset/rilevazione-flusso-veicoli-tramite-spire-anno-2025/information/)
* **Licence:** Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (**CC BY 4.0**)
* **Period Covered:** January 1, 2025 – December 31, 2025 (hourly vehicle count per sensor)

---

### **Dataset Details**
* **Data Granularity:** Counts are provided per hour (columns include `data`, `ora`, `flusso`, along with loop IDs and geographical coordinates).
* **Data Quality Companion:** The portal also publishes complementary accuracy datasets (e.g., *Accuratezza spire anno 2024* / *2025*) detailing operational percentages and counter malfunction indicators for each hourly interval.
